PROFESSIONAL SURVEYING

Precision Starts in the Field.

In Idaho, land surveying underpins property boundaries, development, infrastructure, planning, and construction. Accurate field measurements, dependable mapping, and clear documentation are essential to sound decisions on the ground and in the office. Sawtooth Land Surveying values disciplined field practices, technical accuracy, and professional judgment in every stage of the surveying process.

ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY

Senior Land Surveyor

THE OPPORTUNITY

The Senior Land Surveyor will contribute professional surveying expertise across both field and technical activities, helping support accurate data collection, boundary and property-related work, and project documentation with a strong focus on precision and reliability.

RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Leading or supporting land surveying field operations
  • Collecting accurate field measurements and survey data
  • Performing boundary and property-related surveying work
  • Supporting construction and site layout activities
  • Reviewing field data for accuracy and completeness
  • Working with GPS/GNSS and modern surveying equipment
  • Interpreting survey plans, drawings, and technical documentation
  • Preparing or supporting professional survey documentation
  • Coordinating effectively with field personnel and project teams
  • Maintaining a high standard of accuracy, safety, and professionalism
